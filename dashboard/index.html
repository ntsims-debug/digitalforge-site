      Next Steps — Your Complete Workflow

      • 1Download your ZIP file above
      • 2Load your Prompts CSV into the Prompt Player (in the Tools section below) — it lets you copy each prompt with one click and step through them one by one while you work in your AI image tool
      • 3Generate your artwork using your AI image tool (e.g. Midjourney, Firefly, DALL-E)
      • 4Upscale your generated images for print quality using Upscayl — free download at upscayl.org (recommended: 4× upscale)
      • 5Batch resize to your target print sizes using the Batch Scaler (in the Tools section below) — it slices one master into every size across all supported ratios as JPG + PDF at 300 DPI, organized and zipped. Your master should already be in the ratio you want (Batch Scaler doesn't crop). To crop or adjust an image to the right ratio first, we recommend Photopea (photopea.com) — free, browser-based, no install needed
      • 6Create your product listings using the Listings CSV (e.g. bulk import to Etsy via Vela, or upload manually to Gumroad, Shopify, etc.)
      • 7Add your resized images to each listing and publish
