Getting Started

1. Add a Collection
Click "+ Add Collection" in the sidebar. Name it, choose your niche, and add your product names.
2. Configure Settings
Set your shop name, niche, art style, target buyer, and pricing for the collection.
3. Generate Listings
Click Generate All Listings. Claude AI writes SEO titles, descriptions, 13 tags, and Pinterest captions.
4. Export and Upload
Download your CSV and import into Vela for bulk upload to your platform. Done.
