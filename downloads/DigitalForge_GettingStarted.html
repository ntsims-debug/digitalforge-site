Getting Started
DigitalForge Pro AI Dashboard

Welcome! You're three quick steps from your first collection.

Step by Step
Activate & Set Up
1

Open the Dashboard

Go to digitalforgepro.ai/dashboard in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge on a laptop or desktop computer.

2

Enter Your License Key

On first visit you'll see an activation screen. Paste the license key from your Gumroad receipt (also emailed to you). You only do this once per browser — it's remembered after that.

3

Add Your Claude API Key

The Dashboard uses Claude AI to generate your prompts and listings. Create a free key at console.anthropic.com, then paste it when prompted. Your key is stored locally in your browser and never sent to our servers.

About API costs Claude's API is pay-per-use, billed by Anthropic directly — a typical generation costs about $0.05–0.25 depending on product count and model, separate from your one-time Dashboard purchase.
Make Something
Your First Collection

Fill in your niche, art style, AI image tool, collection name, and creative vision → click Generate → preview and edit your prompts and listings → download everything as a ZIP (Prompts CSV, Listings CSV, and Pinterest descriptions). Three built-in tools — below — handle the steps that come after generating.

✓ Free bonus included Your purchase comes with the Brand Email Setup Guide — a comprehensive walkthrough for setting up a professional branded email address (e.g. hello@yourshop.com) using Cloudflare and Gmail, with no paid email hosting. Download it from your Gumroad receipt.
Built In
Your Workflow Tools

Prompt Player — load your Prompts CSV and copy each prompt with one click as you generate images, without losing your place.

Batch Scaler — turn one master into every standard print size at 300 DPI (JPG, PDF, or both), each carrying an embedded Adobe RGB (1998) profile for accurate print-on-demand color, organized into folders and zipped.

Mockup Studio — stage your prints in real rooms and export listing-ready hero images. Build your own room library (remembered between sessions, with Import/Export) from an included example pack; add your Claude key to unlock AI Room Match, which picks the best room for a print.
